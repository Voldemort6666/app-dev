## About the Series

**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama television series created by *Vince Gilligan*. It follows Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing illegal drugs after being diagnosed with cancer.

The series explores themes of **crime, morality, family, ambition, and consequences**.

## Main Characters

- **Walter White** – A chemistry teacher who becomes a drug manufacturer.
- **Jesse Pinkman** – Walter's former student and business partner.
- **Skyler White** – Walter's wife.
- **Hank Schrader** – A DEA agent and Walter's brother-in-law.
- **Saul Goodman** – A criminal lawyer.
